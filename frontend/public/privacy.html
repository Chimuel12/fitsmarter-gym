FitSmarter privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

FitSmarter is a gym and body-weight tracker. It is built so that your training data belongs to you.

What stays on your device

Your workouts, plans, weigh-ins, measurements and settings are stored on your phone or in your browser. FitSmarter has no server of its own that receives them, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, FitSmarter saves one file, "FitSmarter data.json", in a folder of your own Drive and reads it back to keep your devices in sync. It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can only see files it created or that you picked for it, never the rest of your Drive. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Google Drive, and delete the file in Drive.

Compete with friends (optional)

If you sign in to compete, a summary of each workout (date, duration, number of sets, volume, best lifts and their levels), your display name and profile picture are shared with the groups you join, through Google Firebase. You can leave a group or sign out at any time. Ask us to delete your account and its data at the address below.

Health Connect (optional, Android)

If you turn it on, FitSmarter writes your finished workouts and weigh-ins to Health Connect and reads your weight, body fat and heart rate from it. This data stays on your phone and is used only to show it in the app. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising.

Children

FitSmarter is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/Chimuel12/fitsmarter-gym.
