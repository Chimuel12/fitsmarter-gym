FitSmarter privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

FitSmarter is a free, non-commercial gym and body-weight tracker. It is built so that your training data belongs to you. This policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The data controller is the person who maintains FitSmarter. You can reach them through the contact at the end of this page.

What stays on your device

Your workouts, plans, weigh-ins, measurements, height, age and settings are stored on your phone or in your browser. FitSmarter has no server of its own that receives them, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Google Drive sync

To keep your progress safe, FitSmarter asks you to connect Google Drive before your first workout. It saves one file, "FitSmarter data.json", in a folder of your own Drive and reads it back to keep your devices in sync, when you open the app, when you open the Body screen and after each change. It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can only see files it created or that you picked for it, never the rest of your Drive. The file is yours: only you and Google can read it. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Google Drive and delete the file in Drive. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Compete with friends (optional)

Only if you sign in to compete and agree on the consent screen. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and, as training data can say something about your health, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You must be 16 or older.

  • What your groups see: your display name and, for each workout you finish, its date, duration, number of sets, volume, points and the level of your best lifts. Your profile picture and the weights and reps of your best lifts only if you turn them on (both are off by default). Workouts from before your consent only if you choose to share the last 30 days.
  • Never shared: your body weight, height, age, measurements, notes or full history.
  • Who sees it: only members of the groups you join. Anyone with a group's 6-letter code can join it, so share codes only with people you trust.
  • Where: Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), run by Google as processor. Google may process data outside the EU under the EU standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • How long: each workout summary is deleted after 90 days. Leaving a group deletes everything you shared in it.
  • Your controls, on the Compete screen under "Your privacy": change what is shown, download everything stored about you as a file, or delete your friends account. Deleting removes your profile, every summary and kudos you shared and your sign-in, at once. Your workouts on your device and in Google Drive stay.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your friends account. This does not affect what was shared before.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on rest-timer or reminder notifications in a browser, a small notification server (Cloudflare Workers) keeps your browser's push address, when your rest ends and which days to remind you. It never receives your workouts. Turning notifications off deletes them.

Health Connect (optional, Android)

If you turn it on, FitSmarter writes your finished workouts and weigh-ins to Health Connect and reads your weight, body fat and heart rate from it. This data stays on your phone and is used only to show it in the app. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising.

Children

FitSmarter is not directed at children under 13. Compete with friends is only for people 16 or older.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object, and to withdraw consent (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Most of it you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, use the contact below. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one of the state where you live.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/Chimuel12/fitsmarter-gym.
